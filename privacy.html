OpenPlayer

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Scope & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the OpenPlayer application across all supported platforms, including Android phones and tablets, Android Auto, Android Automotive OS (AAOS), and iOS / iPadOS / CarPlay.

OpenPlayer is an open-source, client-side media player designed exclusively to connect directly to user-operated, self-hosted media servers (such as Jellyfin and Navidrome / OpenSubsonic instances).

Summary: We do not operate user accounts, cloud databases, advertising networks, or tracking services. Your credentials, catalog metadata, and media remain strictly between your device and the server you explicitly configure.

2. Data We Process and Store

All data processed by OpenPlayer is retained locally on your personal device or transmitted directly to your configured media server:

A. Server Authentication Credentials

  • Information stored: Your server URL, username, user ID, and server access tokens (or QuickConnect session authorization).
  • Storage security: On Android and Android Automotive OS, credentials are encrypted at rest using the hardware-backed Android Keystore with AES/GCM/NoPadding. On iOS, credentials are stored securely in the Apple Keychain (kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly).
  • Backup exclusions: Credential stores are marked to exclude automatic cloud backups (Google Drive backup and Apple iCloud device backups).

B. Catalog Metadata & Offline Audio Cache

  • Information stored: Artist, album, track titles, playlist entries, user ratings, play counts, and audio files.
  • Storage purpose: Audio caching enables offline playback and eliminates mobile bandwidth re-downloads in low-connectivity areas or while driving.
  • Location: Stored within app-private internal storage, inaccessible to other applications without root/jailbreak privileges.

C. Car Party Jam Sessions

  • Information processed: Real-time playback status (track ID, position, play/pause state) and queue operations.
  • Session isolation: Host QR codes embed ephemeral session coordination tokens. Guest devices interact via isolated session identifiers and cannot view or extract your master server credentials or passwords.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

OpenPlayer requests only the permissions necessary for media playback and local operation:

  • Foreground Service & Media Playback (Android): Required to keep audio playing uninterrupted when the app is in the background or the screen is locked, in accordance with Android 14+ foreground service policies.
  • Network Access (Internet / Wi-Fi): Required solely to stream and cache audio files and metadata directly from your configured server address.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents the CPU from sleeping during active audio playback.
  • Camera (Optional): Used exclusively to scan Party Jam QR codes or QuickConnect pairing codes. Camera frames are processed entirely in memory in real time; no images, video, or biometric data are recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Local Network (iOS): Enables discovery and connection to personal media servers hosted on your local Wi-Fi or LAN subnet.

4. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

OpenPlayer is committed to complete digital privacy:

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate Google Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, telemetry libraries, or third-party performance monitors.
  • No Crash Trackers: No third-party crash reporting SDKs (such as Sentry or Crashlytics) are embedded. Any diagnostic logs remain on-device.
  • No Advertisements: OpenPlayer contains no ad networks, tracking pixels, or monetization beacons.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT): OpenPlayer does not track users across third-party websites or applications, and does not sell or share user identifiers with data brokers.

5. Direct Server Communication Only

OpenPlayer does not route traffic through proxy servers, relay hubs, or intermediate services operated by the developers. All network requests originate from your device and terminate directly at the endpoint URL you configure in Settings.

Communication with your server adheres to standard secure transport protocols (HTTPS/TLS) as supported and configured on your server.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because OpenPlayer does not maintain user accounts on centralized servers, you have immediate, unilateral control over all your data:

  • Disconnect / Sign Out: You can purge all cached server tokens, library metadata, and cached audio files at any time via Settings → Disconnect Server.
  • Clear App Data / Uninstall: Uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Storage" in system settings immediately deletes all encrypted credentials, database indexes, and cached tracks.

7. Children's Privacy

OpenPlayer does not knowingly collect, transmit, or solicit personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because OpenPlayer collects no personal data from any user, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any revisions to this policy will be committed directly to the open-source repository and posted at this URL (https://openplayer.org/privacy). If modifications change how device permissions or local credentials are treated, notices will be included in the application release notes.
