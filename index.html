OpenPlayer streams bit-perfect audio from your personal Jellyfin and Navidrome servers to your phone and vehicle with native Android Auto and Android Automotive OS (AAOS) support.

View on GitHub Privacy Policy

Self-Hosted Backends

Native integration for Jellyfin and Navidrome / OpenSubsonic instances. Supports QuickConnect PIN pairing and direct streaming without vendor lock-in.

Built for the Cockpit

Certified for Android Auto and standalone Android Automotive OS (AAOS) adhering strictly to Google's Design for Driving guidelines with large 76dp+ touch targets.

Car Party Jam

Passengers scan a QR code on the car display to join as queue controllers in real time—without sharing passwords or swapping Bluetooth connections.

Audiophile DSP Pipeline

True gapless playback, ReplayGain normalization (Track & Album modes with peak limiter), and smart album-aware crossfading.

Dual-Tier Offline Caching

Rolling LRU cache buffers recently played music, while permanent pinned storage protects your favorite albums and playlists from eviction.

100% Private & Open Source

Zero telemetry, zero advertising SDKs, and zero third-party tracking. All server credentials are encrypted with hardware keystores.
